A new house starts as a frame.

Once the builder has the walls and roof up, the electrical can start. It happens in three stages, each one before a different part of the build.

01 · Rough-in

Wire runs through the open studs.

Before drywall, we set the panel and every box, then pull wire for each circuit through the framing. The city inspects it while the walls are still open.

02 · Trim-out

Outlets, switches and fixtures go in.

After drywall and paint we come back to install the devices and light fixtures and make up the panel. This is the part of the job people actually see.

03 · Final

Final inspection, then the power comes on.

Once the final inspection passes, the house gets permanent power and the lights work for the first time. Then it goes to the new owners.
